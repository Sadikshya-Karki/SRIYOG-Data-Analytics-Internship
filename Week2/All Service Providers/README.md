# All Service Providers – Service Provider Count Analysis
**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** June 2026

## About
A mapping of all on-demand home services available in Kathmandu, Nepal, along with the number of service providers offering each service. The dataset covers 64 companies and identifies 360+ unique services, ranking them by provider count to highlight market saturation and competitive intensity across service categories.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|-------------|

## Key Findings
- Electrician or Electrical Service has the highest number of providers (27), making it the most saturated service category
- Plumbing (24), Painting (19), AC Service (17), and Carpentry (16) round out the top five most competitive services
- A large number of services have only 1 provider, indicating niche, low-competition opportunities across many categories
- 64 companies were mapped, offering a combined total of over 360 unique services across Kathmandu
