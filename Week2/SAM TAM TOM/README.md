# SAM TAM TOM – Home Based Services Market Sizing Analysis
**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** July 2026

## About
A market sizing analysis of the home-based services sector in Nepal using the TAM, SAM and TOM framework. The report calculates the total, serviceable, and obtainable market based on official data from the 2021 Census and NLSS IV household survey, with clearly stated assumptions where official data was not available.

## Key Findings
- TAM (Total Addressable Market) is approximately NPR 56.58 billion, based on all 6.67 million households in Nepal
- SAM (Serviceable Available Market) is approximately NPR 21.39 billion, covering only households with internet access (37.8% of total)
- TOM (Target Obtainable Market) is approximately NPR 0.64 billion, based on a 3% early-stage market share assumption
- Internet access is the single biggest factor that reduces the market, removing 62.2% of TAM on its own
- 71% of the model is based on official government sources; only two numbers in the entire model are assumptions
- TOM value scales in a straight line with market share, making it easy to update once real traction data is available

## Data Sources
- Nepal Census 2021 (total households, average household size)
- NLSS IV Household Survey 2022/23 (average annual household expenditure)
