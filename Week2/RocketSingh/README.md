# RocketSingh – Kathmandu Market Analysis

**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** June 2026

## About
Market analysis of 30 home service categories for RocketSingh's entry into
Kathmandu, covering competitor mapping, local pricing, and market saturation
levels across the Kathmandu Valley.

## Key Findings
- 8 categories are Saturated: Plumbing, Electrical, AC Services, Painting, Carpentry, Deep Cleaning, Pest Control, Packing and Moving
- Airbnb Maintenance has only 2 known providers — strong early entry opportunity
- Niche categories with few competitors: Chef at Home, Home Automation, Indoor Planting, Physiotherapy
- Most bookings happen via phone, Facebook, or personal referrals — no dominant digital platform
- Tech disruption opportunities identified in Salon at Home, AC Services, Pest Control, and Deep Cleaning

## Saturation Legend
| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Saturated | Highly competitive; success depends on speed, trust, and pricing |
| Contested | No clear leader; strong execution can win market share |
| Partial | Few players; good opportunity to establish presence |
| Niche | Very few providers; strong early mover opportunity |
