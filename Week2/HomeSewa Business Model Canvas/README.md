# HomeSewa – Business Model Canvas Analysis
**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** June 2026

## About
A practical business model analysis of HomeSewa, Nepal's on-demand hyperlocal home service platform. The report applies the Business Model Canvas framework developed by Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur to break down how HomeSewa creates, delivers, and captures value across nine key building blocks.

## Key Findings
- HomeSewa's core value lies in AI driven matching, verified professionals, and real time alerts through app, SMS, and WhatsApp
- The platform serves four main customer segments: urban homeowners, offices, Airbnb hosts, and event based customers such as brides
- Revenue is generated through booking commissions, a low cost membership fee for professionals, pay per lead charges, and higher value service bookings
- The professional network and AI matching technology are HomeSewa's most critical resources, making investment in these areas the highest priority
- Cost structure is dominated by app development, marketing, communication charges, and partner verification
