# Yearly Services – On-Demand Home Services Demand Analysis
**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** June 2026

## About
Seasonal demand analysis of 40 home and lifestyle services across a full calendar year in Kathmandu, Nepal. The report identifies peak demand months, seasonal patterns, and category-level trends to support workforce planning and service scheduling decisions.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Yearly_Services.xlsx` | Raw dataset |
| `Yearly_Services.ipynb` | Analysis notebook (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn) |
| `Yearly_Services.html` | HTML export of notebook |
| `Yearly_Services.pdf` | Final report |

## Key Findings
- April and May are the busiest months of the year with 22 and 23 High-demand services respectively
- October and November form a strong secondary peak driven by post-monsoon repairs and Dashain/Tihar preparations
- Bridal Makeup and RO Water Purifying are the most consistently in-demand services, each recording High demand across 6 months
- Tech and Smart Home is the most year-round resilient category — no service recorded Low demand in any month
- Construction and Renovation is the most weather-sensitive category, dropping sharply during monsoon months
- Wellness and Beauty follows two distinct calendars: wedding/festival season and winter comfort season
- Outdoor and Garden is the most narrowly seasonal category, averaging only 2.7 peak months per service
