## About
A curated list of 250 home and lifestyle services, matched against availability across five providers — RocketSingh, HomeSewa, Tackles, BuildingSewa, and GardenSewa.

## Key Findings
- 250 total services across 25 categories
- Most common category: Cleaning Services (48 services)
- BuildingSewa offers the most services (43 of 250)
- RocketSingh and HomeSewa each offer 30 of the 250 services
- Tackles offers 29 of the 250 services
- GardenSewa offers 21 of the 250 services
