# ILO Nepal 2025 in Numbers Analysis
Prepared for: Sriyog Consulting | Date: July, 2026

## About
This project analyzes the "2025 in Numbers" infographic (page 3) from the ILO Country Office for Nepal's Annual Report 2025, covering Social Security Fund enrolment, Employment Service Centres, enterprise labour audits, migrant worker outreach, child labour elimination and entrepreneurship training, along with the 2025 minimum wage increase. The goal is to visualize the scale of these results, examine gender participation patterns where reported, and highlight where implementation appears strongest or still partial.

## Key Findings
- Social Security Fund enrolment nearly quadrupled between 2023 and 2025, from 0.6 million to 2.26 million workers.
- Employment Service Centre job placement conversion is low relative to registration, at approximately 8.7%.
- Where gender data is reported, women's participation is strong, 58% in Employment Service Centre registration and 79% in entrepreneurship training.
- Child Labour Free Zone declarations lag far behind engagement, only 14 of 204 engaged local governments have completed formal declaration.
- The minimum wage increased 13% to NPR 19,550 per month, applying uniformly across all sectors.
- Not all metrics on the infographic include a gender breakdown; only Employment Service Centre registration and entrepreneurship training report this detail.
