# Ratings and Reviews
**Prepared by:** Sadikshya Karki | **Date:** July 2026

## About
Google Maps ratings and review counts for 87 home service companies in Nepal.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Ratings_and_Reviews.xlsx` | Company list with website, Google Maps rating, and number of reviews |

## Key Findings
- 87 total companies checked
- 68 companies have a Google Maps rating
- 13 companies have no Google Maps listing found
- 6 companies are listed but not yet rated
- Average rating: 4.758823529
- Total combined reviews: 8,375
- Royal Cleaning Services has the most reviews (1,515)
- 25 companies hold a perfect 5.0 rating
