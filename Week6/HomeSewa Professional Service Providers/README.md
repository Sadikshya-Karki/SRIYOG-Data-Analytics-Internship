# Professional Service Providers - Kathmandu Valley

**Prepared by:** Sadikshya Karki | **Date:** July 2026

## About
A researched directory of individual and business service providers across 30 home and property service categories in the Kathmandu Valley, compiled through structured Google Maps-based research across Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, and Lalitpur.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|--------------|
| `HomeSewa_Professional_Service_Providers.xlsx` | Final dataset of 459 service provider listings across 30 categories |

## Key Findings
- 459 provider listings collected across 30 service categories in Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, and Lalitpur
- Most represented category: Salon at Home (38 listings), followed by Physiotherapy and Indoor Planting (30 each)
- Kathmandu accounts for the largest share of listings (260), followed by Lalitpur (120) and Bhaktapur (73)
- 3 categories — Chef at Home, EV Charger Installation, and Airbnb Maintenance — returned no individual or business listings on Google Maps despite repeated search attempts

## Methodology
- Searched Google Maps for each service category across all three cities, prioritizing independent/individual providers over registered businesses where available
- Collected provider name, city, contact number, rating, review count, and source link for each listing
- Applied consistent criteria to distinguish genuine individual providers from businesses and training centers
- Standardized provider names for formatting consistency (casing, punctuation) without altering the underlying business names
- Left categories with no available listings marked accordingly rather than omitting them from the dataset
