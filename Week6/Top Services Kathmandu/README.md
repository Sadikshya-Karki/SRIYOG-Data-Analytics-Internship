# Top 30 Services - Kathmandu

**Prepared by:** Sadikshya Karki | **Date:** July 2026

## About
A curated shortlist of the 30 highest-demand home and property services for the Kathmandu market, narrowed down from a master catalog of 150+ services across six providers — Tackles, GardenSewa, HomeSewa, CleaningSewa, RocketSingh, and Building Sewa.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|--------------|

## Key Findings
- 30 services shortlisted from a combined catalog of 150+ across 6 providers
- Most represented category: Cleaning Services (9 of 30)
- Repairs & Handyman services form the second-largest group (8 of 30)
- Demand notes are grounded in structural, year-round factors rather than seasonal or festival-based spikes — e.g. water shortages, hard water quality, aging infrastructure, the ongoing apartment construction boom, high rental turnover, and remittance-funded home upgrades

## Methodology
- Consolidated overlapping service listings across all six provider catalogs
- Used repetition across providers as a signal of core, high-demand services
- Excluded niche or low-frequency services not relevant to the Kathmandu market
