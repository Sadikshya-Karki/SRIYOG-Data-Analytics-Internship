# HR Recruitment Performance Analysis

Prepared for: Sriyog Consulting | Date: July, 2026

## About
This project analyzes recruitment and performance data for 200 employees, covering department, job title, gender, experience, monthly salary, performance score, attendance, annual bonus, employment status and training hours. The goal is to understand how these different aspects relate to each other, such as whether more experience leads to higher pay, and whether performance and attendance connect to pay or training at all.

## Key Findings
- The dataset is clean, with no missing values, duplicates or placeholder values found anywhere.
- Employees are spread evenly across 10 departments, and gender is exactly balanced at 100 male and 100 female.
- Executives and Senior Executives are the largest job title groups.
- Monthly salary and annual bonus are both strongly tied to experience, more experience means more pay.
- Performance score and attendance percentage do not meaningfully relate to salary, bonus or experience.
- Training hours are highest for newer employees and drop off as experience grows.
