# HomeSewa Training Providers - Fee Structure
Prepared for: Sriyog Consulting | Date: July, 2026

## About
This project extends the HomeSewa Training Providers dataset with a Fee Structure column, classifying each provider's training as Free or Paid across 30 service categories relevant to HomeSewa's on-demand home services in Kathmandu. Classification was based on researching each provider's website, course pages, or verified reporting on their funding model.

## Key Findings
- Of 246 providers across 30 service categories, 237 are Paid and 8 are Free; Packing & Moving (1 entry) has no data to classify.
- The 8 Free providers are all NGO, donor, or tender-funded programs rather than commercial academies: Olgapuri Vocational School, Action For Development Pvt. Ltd., National Rehabilitation Society For the Disabled, F-Skill Nepal, ISET Nepal, TITAN Nepal, and Chandra Surya Sip Bikash Sanstha (listed twice, under Salon at Home and Refrigerator Repair).
- Masonry Repair has the highest concentration of free training (4 of 13 providers), reflecting donor-funded earthquake-resilient construction and disaster-recovery programs in that category.
- Salon at Home, Handyman, Pest Control and Refrigerator Repair each have exactly one free provider; every other category is 100% Paid.
- The overwhelming majority of providers (237 of 246, over 96%) are private businesses charging tuition, confirming that HomeSewa's training-provider landscape is dominated by commercial academies and institutes rather than subsidized programs.
- Free providers cluster around social-mission categories (disability inclusion, disaster resilience, marginalized-community upskilling) rather than beauty, hospitality, or home-automation training, which are exclusively fee-based.
