# HomeSewa Partnership Companies

Prepared for: HomeSewa | Date: July, 2026

## About

This project catalogs 114 partner businesses across 30 home-service categories that HomeSewa works with, including salon, massage, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, repair and renovation services. Each entry lists the service category, company name, contact number and a link to their Google Maps listing. The goal is to keep a single, organized reference of verified service partners for sourcing and outreach.

## Key Findings

- The list covers 114 partner companies spread across 30 service categories, from Salon at Home and Bridal Makeup to CCTV Services and Home Renovation.
- Pest Control and Massage Therapy have the most listed partners (7 each), followed by Plumbing and CCTV Services (6 each).
- Chef at Home, EV Charger Installation, Airbnb Maintenance, Home Renovation, Garden Care and Masonry Repair each have only 1 partner listed, marking them as thin categories that need more sourcing.
- Chef at Home, EV Charger Installation and Airbnb Maintenance have no partner data at all (company, contact and link are blank), so these categories are effectively unfilled.
- Almost all listings (111 of 114) are sourced from Google Maps, making it the primary channel used to find partners.
- Contact numbers are inconsistently formatted, some are standard 10-digit mobile numbers while a few appear to be landline or malformed entries, so this column would benefit from cleanup.
