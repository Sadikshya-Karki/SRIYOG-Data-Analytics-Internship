Data Quality Check¶

In [6]:
cat_cols = ['Department','Gender','Job_Role','Promotion_Status','Work_Location']
for c in cat_cols:
    print(c, ':', (df[c]=='Unknown').sum(), 'Unknown rows')

print()
print('Salary below Rs 5,000:', (df['Monthly_Salary']<5000).sum())
print('Salary above Rs 5,00,000:', (df['Monthly_Salary']>500000).sum())

Department : 9 Unknown rows
Gender : 9 Unknown rows
Job_Role : 8 Unknown rows
Promotion_Status : 9 Unknown rows
Work_Location : 9 Unknown rows

Salary below Rs 5,000: 3
Salary above Rs 5,00,000: 9

What this means: "Unknown" shows up in 5 different columns (Department, Gender, Job_Role, Promotion_Status, Work_Location) on different rows each time, about 43 rows in total. These are kept in the dataset as their own category, and only excluded from the specific chart where that column is being analyzed, instead of dropping the row everywhere.

A few salary values also look like data entry mistakes (3 below Rs 5,000, 9 above Rs 5,00,000, up to Rs 50 lakh). These are excluded only from salary-based charts and averages below, not deleted from the dataset.
