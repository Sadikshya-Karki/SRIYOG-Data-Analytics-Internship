# HomeSewa Service Pricing Analysis

**Prepared by:** Sadikshya Karki | **Date:** July 2026

## About
A competitor pricing benchmark across HomeSewa's home service categories, compiled through structured Google Maps and web-based research to identify what comparable providers in Nepal charge for each service. For every service, three competitor companies were identified along with their starting price and contact details, to support HomeSewa in setting its own competitive pricing.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|--------------|
| `HomeSewa_Service_Pricing_Analysis.xlsx` | Pricing comparison across 30 service categories, with 3 competitors and starting prices each |

## Key Findings
- 30 service categories, each benchmarked against 3 competitors (90 listings total)
- Doorsteps Nepal is the most frequent competitor, appearing across 16 of 30 services
- Nearly half of competitor prices (44/90) are marked "Not Disclosed"
- `HomeSewa Price` column is currently blank — competitor data only

## Methodology
- Searched Google for competitor home-service providers relevant to each service category
- Collected up to 3 competitors per service with starting price and contact number(s)
- Marked price as "Not Disclosed" where a competitor doesn't publish rates
