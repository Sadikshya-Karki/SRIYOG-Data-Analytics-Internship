# GardenSewa Training Providers Analysis

**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** August 2026

## About
Analysis of Nepal-based training providers offering training relevant to GardenSewa's service categories, covering 41 training listings from 13 distinct training providers, centers, and institutes operating primarily in Kathmandu Valley, examining service coverage, provider breadth, provider type distribution, and fee structure.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|--------------|
| `Training_Providers.xlsx` | Raw dataset |
| `Training_Providers.ipynb` | Full report with introduction, analysis, visualizations, and key findings |
| `Training_Providers_Report.html` | HTML export of .ipynb file |

## Key Findings
- No single training provider covers all 13 GardenSewa services; the broadest provider, Organic Farm Nepal, covers 8 of 13
- Landscape Lighting (7 providers) and Hardscaping (6 providers) are the best-served service categories
- Lawn Care and Flower Maintenance are the most underserved, with only 1 training provider each
- Training is almost exclusively paid, accounting for 97.6% of listings (40 of 41); only 1 free training option exists, offered by World Link Technical Training Institute for Landscape Lighting
- Provider types are fairly balanced: Training Centers (16 listings), Training Providers (13 listings), and Training Institutes (12 listings)
- Organic Farm Nepal, Green Smith Nepal, HASERA Permaculture Learning Center, and Kumari Flora are the anchor providers, together accounting for 25 of 41 listings
