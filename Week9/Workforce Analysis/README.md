# GardenSewa Workforce Analysis

**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** August 2026

## About
Workforce-side analysis of the 20 garden service categories covered in the GardenSewa Comparative Analysis, assessing provider supply, demand level, competition intensity, and provider-type mix to identify saturated services and underserved market gaps.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Workforce_Analysis.xlsx` | Dataset with Provider Found, Demand Level, Competition Level, Rank, provider-type %, and Supply Status, linked to the Comparative Analysis workbook |
| `Workforce_Analysis.ipynb` | Full report with introduction, analysis, visualizations, and key findings |
| `Workforce_Analysis_Report.html` | HTML export of .ipynb file |

## Key Findings
- 11 of 20 services (55%) are classified as "Limited" supply, compared to only 4 "Well-served" and 5 "Sufficient"
- Pest and Disease Control is the most saturated category with 18 providers, both High demand and Well-served
- Soil Improvement, Plant Propagation, Rooftop Gardening, and Artificial Gardening each have only 1 provider
- Irrigation Services, Seasonal Services, and Rooftop Gardening combine Medium demand with Low competition, marking them as the clearest underserved opportunities
- Organized companies serve 11 of 20 services exclusively, with no app-based or informal presence at all
- App/marketplace platforms have a foothold in only 5 services, always as a minority share alongside formal companies
- Competition Level and Supply Status produce identical groupings, since both are derived from the same Provider Found thresholds
