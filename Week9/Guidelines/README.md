# Guidelines for New Data Analyst Interns

**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** August 2026

## About
A two-page guide written for newly joined data analyst interns, based on real situations faced during the internship. It covers how to approach research, dataset planning, Python-based analysis, report writing, communication, time management, and adapting to new tools, with the goal of helping new interns settle in faster and work more effectively from day one.

## Key Sections
- **Start with strong, structured research** — Review multiple sources, keep raw notes before building a dataset, and mark information as unavailable instead of guessing
- **Plan your dataset before you build it** — Decide the column structure in advance and work in smaller batches to keep large datasets accurate
- **Let Python do the heavy lifting** — Structure Jupyter notebooks with an introduction, chart-level insights, and a key findings section, then export as PDF or HTML for easy sharing
- **Write reports people actually want to read** — Use headings, keep language simple, and explain why a trend matters instead of restating the dataset
- **Communicate early, communicate often** — Share regular progress updates and be specific about obstacles rather than saying things are fine
- **Manage your time by breaking tasks down** — Split large projects into daily targets and keep a simple daily log
- **Stay open to learning new tools** — Try learning new platforms independently first before asking for help
