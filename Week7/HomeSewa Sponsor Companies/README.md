# Sponsor Companies - HomeSewa Service Categories

**Prepared by:** Sadikshya Karki | **Date:** August 2026

## About
A researched directory of manufacturer and trading companies relevant to HomeSewa's home service categories, compiled through structured Google Maps-based research across Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, Lalitpur, and other cities in Nepal. The goal is to identify companies that produce or trade the products used within each service (e.g., paint manufacturers for Painting, pipe suppliers for Plumbing) as potential sponsors.

## Files

## Key Findings
- 263 unique manufacturer/trading companies collected across 22 service categories
- Most represented category: Painting (30 companies), followed by Indoor Planting (26) and Carpentry (25)
- Kathmandu accounts for the largest share of listings (79), followed by Lalitpur (27) and Bhaktapur (26)
- Several companies serve more than one service category due to overlapping product needs (e.g., cosmetics suppliers listed under Salon at Home, Bridal Makeup, and Spa at Home; general appliance wholesalers listed under Washing Machine Repair, Refrigerator Repair, and AC Services) — these are combined into a single row with all applicable services noted, rather than repeated as separate entries
- 8 categories — Chef at Home, Physiotherapy, Home Renovation, Packing and Moving, Airbnb Maintenance, EV Charger Installation, and Modular Kitchen — returned no clear manufacturer/trading company match on Google Maps, or are covered by the product categories of other services (e.g., Home Renovation overlaps fully with Tiling, Painting, Masonry Repair, Carpentry, and Electrical Repairs)

## Methodology
- Searched Google Maps for manufacturer, supplier, wholesaler, distributor, and trader companies relevant to each service category's core product(s)
- Collected company name, company type, contact number, city, and source link for each listing
- Applied consistent criteria to exclude retail stores and service-only providers, keeping only producing or trading companies (per assigned task scope)
- Cross-checked ambiguous Google Maps category labels against customer reviews and product descriptions to confirm the correct product match before including a company
- Standardized company names for formatting consistency (casing, punctuation) without altering the underlying business names
- Combined services for companies whose products apply to more than one category, rather than duplicating rows
