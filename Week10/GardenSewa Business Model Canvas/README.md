# GardenSewa – Business Model Canvas Analysis
**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** August 2026

## About
A practical business model analysis of GardenSewa, a professional gardening services platform based in Kathmandu, Nepal. The report applies the Business Model Canvas framework developed by Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur to break down how GardenSewa creates, delivers, and captures value across nine key building blocks.

## Key Findings
- GardenSewa's core value lies in proven expertise, a strong track record of completed garden transformations, and a full range of services from one time cleanups to yearly maintenance contracts
- The platform serves five main customer segments: urban homeowners, hotels and resorts, offices and corporate clients, Airbnb hosts, and home growers in the partnership programme
- Revenue is generated through one time service fees, recurring maintenance contracts, landscape design and hardscaping projects, plant and soil sales, and commission from the partnership programme
- The skilled gardening workforce and established brand reputation are GardenSewa's most important resources, supported by its network of hotel, resort, and corporate clients
- Cost structure is dominated by gardener wages and training, plant and material procurement, equipment, and marketing
