# GardenSewa Ratings and Reviews Analysis

**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** August 2026

## About
Analysis of Google Maps ratings and reviews for companies offering GardenSewa-type services and related training in Nepal, covering 24 service providers and 13 training providers operating mainly in Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur, examining rating levels, review counts, and how the two groups compare in terms of customer feedback.

## Key Findings
- Among the 24 service providers, 23 have rating data available; 7 hold a perfect 5 star rating, and the group average rating is about 4.6 out of 5
- Among the 13 training providers, 11 have rating data available; 5 hold a perfect 5 star rating, and the group average rating is about 4.8 out of 5, slightly higher than service providers
- Service providers draw far more customer reviews overall, with Pest Defence Service (1,148 reviews) and Orange Ball (1,110 reviews) each crossing 1,000, well above any training provider
- Nikhil Multi Services Pvt. Ltd. is the most reviewed training provider, with 559 reviews at a 4.9 rating
- Several companies hold a 5 star rating but have very few reviews, such as Hamro Nursery (3 reviews) and Organic Farm Nepal (1 review), meaning their rating reflects only a small number of customers
- The lowest rated companies in each group are Smart Care among service providers (3.9) and Kumari Flora among training providers (3.9)
- 3 companies had no rating or review data on Google Maps at all: Aadarsh Pest Control, Global Reliance Training Center Pvt. Ltd., and Green Smith Nepal
- Companies with both a high rating and a high review count, such as Orange Ball, Pest Defence Service, Technical Sewa, Nikhil Multi Services, and Skills Nepal, stand out as the strongest performers in customer trust and reach
