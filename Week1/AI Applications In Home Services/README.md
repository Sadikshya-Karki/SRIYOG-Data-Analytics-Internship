## About
Slide deck examining how AI is applied across three global home service
platforms — Urban Company, Snabbit, and TaskRabbit — and how those
applications can be used by RocketSingh (Chennai, India).

## Key Findings
- Global market projected to grow from USD 3.71B (2021) to USD 14.7B by 2030
- Asia Pacific is the fastest growing region at 19.2% CAGR
- Urban Company uses AI for service matching; Snabbit for worker dispatch; TaskRabbit for tasker recommendation
- TaskRabbit launched AI booking through Claude in April 2026
- Common limitation across all three: high AI development costs
