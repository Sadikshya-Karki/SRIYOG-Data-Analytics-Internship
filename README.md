# SRIYOG Data Analytics Internship

Data analytics internship completed at SRIYOG Consulting (June 21 – August 28, 2026, 60 working days). The project covers ten weeks of market research and analysis for two Kathmandu-based service platforms — **HomeSewa** (on-demand home services) and, from Week 8 onward, **GardenSewa** (gardening services) — plus a content-writing sprint on data analytics blog articles.

## About the Project

Each week focuses on a specific research or analysis task for HomeSewa or GardenSewa: mapping services to providers, professions, and training institutes; competitor and pricing analysis; demand forecasting; business modelling; workforce analysis; ratings and reviews research; and more. Work is delivered as structured Excel datasets, Jupyter Notebook analyses (exported to HTML/PDF), presentation decks, and written reports.

## Objective

Build a reliable, structured market-research foundation for HomeSewa and GardenSewa by collecting, cleaning, and analyzing real market data, then apply that same research methodology consistently across two different service verticals.

## Workflow

1. **Market Research** – Identified companies, professions, and training providers offering each assigned service category, sourced primarily from Google, Google Maps, and provider websites.
2. **Data Cleaning & Structuring** – Organized raw research findings into structured Excel datasets with consistent fields (service, company, contact, location, rating, source link, etc.).
3. **Data Analysis & Visualization** – Analyzed datasets in Jupyter Notebooks using pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to identify demand trends, market gaps, and competitive positioning, then exported findings as HTML/PDF reports.
4. **Business & Strategic Analysis** – Built supporting frameworks including Business Model Canvases, TAM/SAM/TOM market sizing, and workforce supply-demand modelling.
5. **Reporting & Presentation** – Compiled weekly findings into written reports and, for GardenSewa, a consolidated presentation deck.
