# GardenSewa Comparative Analysis

**Prepared for:** Sriyog Consulting | **Date:** August 2026

## About
Comparison of 20 garden service categories across 92 provider listings from 25 companies operating across Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur, examining competition intensity, provider type distribution, and geographic availability.

## Files
| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Comparative_Analysis.xlsx` | Raw dataset |
| `Comparative_Analysis.ipynb` | Full report with introduction, analysis, visualizations, and key findings |
| `Comparative_Analysis_Report.html` | HTML export of .ipynb file |

## Key Findings
- Pest and Disease Control is the most competitive category with 18 providers
- Soil Improvement, Plant Propagation, Rooftop Gardening, and Artificial Gardening have only 1 provider each
- Organized companies dominate at 89.2%; app platforms and individual providers each make up only 5.4%
- Kathmandu accounts for 79.6% of all listings; Bhaktapur is the most underserved with coverage in only 3 services
- Ajima Nursery (13 listings) and KTM Nursery (10 listings) are the market leaders in service breadth
- Top 4 companies account for approximately 44% of all listings; 12 of 25 companies appear only once
